# Spotify-listeners-behavior
This project analyzes Spotify listening behavior in 2026 to understand listener engagement. It explores which genres, songs, subscription plans, and times of day are associated with the most listening sessions. It also identifies the most-streamed artists, albums, and songs to better understand listening preferences and patterns.

BUSINESS OBJECTIVES
- Know the total listening time in 2026
- Identify total listening records in 2026
- Identify total unique listeners in 2026
- Identify the average listening time per session
- Identify growth of plan listeners from 2025 to 2026
- Identify top genre in 2026
- Determine what time of the day have most listening session
- Identify what age group, plan, and country from the new listeners
- Identify total listeners per plan.

TOOLS AND TECHNOLOGIES
	Power BI – For data visualization, dashboard development, and DAX measures for calculating metrics. PostgreSQL – For data cleaning, data extraction, and SQL-based data analysis.


DASHBOARD PAGE OVERVIEW - The dashboard can answer, "Is the total listeners increase from 2025 to 2026?, what plan have the most listeners?, What time of the day are listening engaged to?". Dashboard gives us details of behaviors of listeners for the year of 2026 including the listeners top album of 2026, genre ranking, top song per age group, growth of listeners number per plan and so on.

INSIGHT 
1. Most of the new listeners in 2026 avail family and premium plan for their listening experience.

2. Unique listeners increased from 323 in 2025 to 349 in 2026, an 8.05% increase. However, total listening sessions declined from 799 to 796, a decrease of approximately 0.38%. Average sessions per listener consequently fell from 2.47 to 2.28. This suggests that the audience expanded, but listening activity did not grow at the same pace.

3. Classical accounted for 47.49% of listening sessions and reached 71.63% of unique listeners in 2026. It also had the highest average sessions per listener at 1.51. In contrast, Rock had the highest average rating at 3.51 and the strongest reported year-over-year session growth at 69.05%.
This suggests that the genre with the greatest reach is not necessarily the genre with the highest average rating or fastest growth.

4. 8:00 AM recorded the most listening activity, at 50 sessions. It also had the highest additional-session percentage at 14.00%. Further analysis showed that 6 out of 43 unique listeners at that hour had multiple sessions, representing 13.95% of its listeners.
This indicates that 8:00 AM was a particularly active period in the dataset, with a small group of listeners contributing multiple sessions.

5. The top five albums accounted for 11.30% of listening sessions in 2026. Each had the same number of sessions and unique listeners, and your listener-level check showed one session per listener for each album.
This indicates that these albums' session totals were driven by distinct listeners rather than multiple album sessions from the same listener in the observed year.

2. Investigate what distinguishes returning, new, and inactive listeners. Monitor sessions per listener activity to see if there are improvement for the whole listening sessions.

4. Look into what listeners play (genre, song) during busy hours, especially at 8:00 AM. We could create and test playlist recommendations during these hours to boost possible streaming.
